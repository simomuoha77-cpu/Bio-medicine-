Purpose of the AI Chatbot

The AI assistant is designed to help medical students learn faster, get answers to academic questions, and simplify access to medical knowledge.

  • Answer medical and academic questions instantly
  • Explain complex medical concepts simply
  • Support exam preparation
  • Provide clinical pearls and study tips
  • Available 24/7 — no waiting for lectures
